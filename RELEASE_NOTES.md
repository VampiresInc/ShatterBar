# ShatterBar 0.1.14 — initial release

Compact profession bars for disenchanting, prospecting, milling, and rogue lockpicking.

- Bind one key to the first available item, or use separate profession bindings.
- Pick a lock, then click the unlocked box again to open and loot directly from the bar.
- Configure visibility, appearance, disenchanting filters, and ignored item types in the native AddOns settings.
- Optional Retail Warband Bank ore/herb support while live targets are available through the bank or profession window.
- Automatic client language, English fallback, and overrides for every included translation.

Choose the ZIP matching your client. Warband processing is experimental and defaults off.
