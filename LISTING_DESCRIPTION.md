# ShatterBar

**Compact profession bars for disenchanting, prospecting, milling, and lockpicking.**

ShatterBar puts eligible items into small movable bars, so you can process them
without hunting through your bags. Click an item or assign a key to the first
available item. Made by Arametheus as a companion to Mailwright.

- Automatically detects your learned professions and processing recipes.
- Shows material counts and available batches.
- Unlock a rogue lockbox, then click again to open and loot it from the bar.
- Filters disenchanting candidates and lets you ignore item types.
- Configurable size, text, growth direction, and visibility.
- All settings live in the native AddOns options.
- English plus ten translated client locales, with automatic selection or an override.
- Optional experimental Retail Warband Bank materials while the game exposes live targets.

Type /sb to open settings. Shift-drag a profession icon to move its bar.
One click or key press performs one action or batch; combat and casting pause processing.

Retail workflows have been tested in game. Forever, Classic Era, Mists Classic,
and Burning Crusade Anniversary packages are available for testing, with in-game
validation still pending. Translations await native-speaker review.

Warband processing defaults off and may require keeping Jewelcrafting or Inscription
open. Short stacks are not automatically combined. Item and spell tooltips use the
client language even when the addon language is overridden.
